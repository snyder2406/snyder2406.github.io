There Are No Rules: Hardening a Custom Embedded Military OS Without a Compliance Baseline

Co-authored the first government-initiated STIG for a custom embedded military operating system with non traditional architecture. Mapped NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 and CIS Ubuntu 24.04 controls to 26 XCCDF rules, validated in STIG Viewer 3.7. Work completed with AFRL / Griffiss Institute, VICEROY MAVEN.
